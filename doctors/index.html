How our doctors work

One medical team, from diagnosis to final result

  1. Diagnosis first

    Our dermatologists and doctors examine the causes of your hair loss before recommending any treatment.

  2. A plan for you

    Your hairline, graft count and treatment plan are designed around your age, face shape and donor area.

  3. Exclusively by doctors

    Extraction and implantation are carried out by doctors, never delegated to technicians.

  4. Follow-up

    The same team reviews your progress and supports existing hair with targeted treatments.
