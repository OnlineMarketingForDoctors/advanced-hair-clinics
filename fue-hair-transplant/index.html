      FUE Hair Transplant

      What it is

      FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction), a method of hair transplantation involving the transfer of individual hair follicles, was first applied in 2003 and has since been continuously refined and perfected.

      Recognized as the most advanced method of hair implantation, it has become the leading choice worldwide due to its minimally invasive nature.

      The advanced FUE technique entails the careful extraction of individual hair follicles from the donor area, followed by their direct implantation into the recipient area without the need for incisions (holes). This approach significantly minimises the risk of injury while ensuring precision for the surgeon during the procedure.

      Despite its advantages, FUE remains a demanding and complex technique for the doctors who perform it. Achieving successful results requires considerable expertise from the medical team, gained through years of practice and experience in managing diverse cases with different requirements.

      FUE Hair Transplant

      Advantages

      The advantages of FUE hair transplantation over the older FUT or Strip technique are numerous and significant, contributing to the establishment of the former and the gradual displacement of the latter. Here’s why and how it outperforms the older methods.

      FUEvsFUT / Strip
      1. Is minimally invasive, it is performed under local anesthesia, has minimum downtime and allows quick recovery.
      2. Leads to minimal or even no bleeding.
      3. Does not leave any permanent linear scars in the donor area.
      4. Has minimum or even no postoperative pain.
      5. Requires no sutures thus the chances of infections are minimized.
      6. Enables uniform use of the donor area.
      7. Allows the accurate calculation of the extracted hair follicles and the reuse of the donor area for a future operation.
      8. Leads to minimal complications (i.e. facial swelling).
      9. Allows hair extraction from body areas such as chest, back or beard without leaving any linear scars behind.
      10. Is suitable both for limited (e.g. eyebrow) and large body areas, without scarring. On the contrary, FUT- Strip method necessitates a skin’s strip removal and application of sutures even in small areas.

        Questions & answers

        FAQs

        Everything you need to know about FUE hair transplantation, answered by our medical team.

        Ask your own question

        Which are the main indicators for a person to get a hair transplant?

        The main indicators are:

        • Male and female androgenetic alopecia
        • Cicatricial alopecia, stable and of small extent, such as Lichen Planopilaris (LPP) and scarring alopecia
        • Eyebrow hair restoration
        • Facial (beard, moustache) hair restoration
        • Scars (derived from a former FUT-Strip session) restoration

        Which are the factors that should be taken into account prior to FUE hair transplant?

        One of the most crucial factors that define the success of hair transplantation lies in the plastic surgeon’s experience in the hairline design and distribution of hair follicles during implantation. Furthermore, essential factors that one should take into consideration prior to hair transplant are the following ones:

        • Patient’s age
        • Patient’s facial features
        • Patient’s grade and extent of alopecia
        • Patient’s donor area availability
        • Patient’s expectations
        • Patient’s prospects for a future hair transplant operation

        What are the aims of a successful hair transplant session?

        The design and the distribution of hair follicles during a hair transplant session with FUE method, is a completely personalized procedure, as it should respond to each patient’s needs. Moreover, the following basic conditions should be fulfilled:

        • Have the minimum possible effects on the donor and recipient sites (no linear scars or marks, uniform hair coverage)
        • Provide a completely natural-looking result
        • Result in the maximum possible density, taking into consideration the duration of the session, the availability of hair follicles and the patient’s needs.

        When the above mentioned specifications are met during an FUE hair transplant operation then, the success rate is quite high, exceeding 95-98% in cases performed by Advanced Hair Clinics.

        Which are the stages followed during a FUE hair transplant session?
        1. Pre-operatively, the donor area is defined and local anesthesia is applied.
        2. Hair follicles are extracted one-by-one using an automated FUE extracting device.
        3. The grafts are temporarily stored for up to 6 hours in a moist and cool environment of 2 to 4 degrees.
        4. Design and mark up of the area where the hair follicles will be implanted.
        5. Implantation of the grafts by using a specialized implanting device.

        How is the implantation performed?

        The hair follicles one-by-one implantation can be performed in one of two ways:

        • By creating reception holes in which transplants are going to be implanted. The reception holes are created either with a needle or with a scalpel, which is a long-established method. The majority of hair transplantation clinics, regardless of whether they apply FUE, Strip or FUT method, still use the pre-mentioned old implantation technique. According to this method, the first step is the creation of hair follicle’s reception holes. Then, the implantation follows by using forceps or the implanter. This technique is frequently followed by significant bleeding and swelling, often accompanied by an unnatural looking result. This outcome derives from the fact that the doctor cannot accurately control, when forceps are being used for transplant implantation, the angle and direction of the hair follicles implantation.
        • By automatically implanting the follicles without creating reception holes. The implantation is performed by using an implanter device. In this way no wounds or scars are caused in the recipient area and the hair growth is naturally looking. This derives from the fact that the implanter is adjusted with respect to the specific area that the implantation is being performed, allowing for the proper control of the angle, depth and direction of the implantation process.

        In Advanced Hair Clinics, Dr Anastasios Vekris, MD, Plastic Surgeon, and his medical team apply One Step Implantation, that does not necessitate the creation of reception holes and leads to excellent results.

        Has the FUE hair transplantation complications?

        The hair transplantation complications are more frequent when FUT-Strip method is being applied, because of the latter’s invasive nature. In FUE hair transplantation the complications are minimal and include:

        • Folliculitis situated in the recipient area (<0.3%)
        • Edema on the forehead that subsides within 1-2 days (<1%)
        • Limited growth (growth rate >90%)
        • Shock loss after the implantation

        In which other cases is FUE applied?

        The Follicular Unit Extraction is applied successfully in:

        • Eyebrow transplant. Many women end up with permanent thinning in their eyebrows because of excessive waxing or laser hair removal. The Follicular Unit Extraction method is the ideal way to overcome this problem. Eyebrow transplant provides the best possible and natural-looking results. Therefore, one has no need to use a micro pigmentation tattoo to cover a potential hair loss in the eyebrows’ area.
        • Facial hair restoration. One might wish to enhance one’s facial hair (i.e. beard transplant) either because of a past injury that has affected the hair density of the area, or due to personal need to obtain thick beard. The Follicular Unit Extraction hair transplant method once more provides the best possible results when a specialised and experienced medical team applies it.
        • FUT scars’ restoration. Former FUT-Strip hair transplant operations quite commonly result in permanent and ugly scars. In order to repair those scars, a great number of people undergo an FUE hair transplant operation. In Advanced Hair Clinics, a great number of hair transplant operations are required to remedy problems caused by these FUT / Strip techniques.

        How is the Unshaven FUE Hair Transplant Session performed?

        The Follicular Unit Extraction method usually requires the shaving of a small part of the donor area. The hair’s length should be 2-3 mm, so as to assist the extraction of the hair follicles. In many cases, though, professional, social or personal reasons do not allow for a short haircut in preparation for hair transplantation. For those that would prefer not to reveal that they have had hair transplantation, having a short haircut could be an issue.

        Dr Anastasios Vekris, MD, Plastic Surgeon, assisted by his medical team, constitute the only specialized team of experts in Greece that can apply unshaven FUE hair transplant sessions, even in challenging cases. During the unshaven session, the doctor extracts the transplants one-by-one in between of the donor area’s long hairs and then transplants them accordingly.

        After the completion of the session, the hair length is still the same as it was before the session and there are no signs of the operation. The unshaven hair transplant session is very common among artists, show business professionals, businessmen, politicians and VIPs. Moreover, it is the ideal method for those that have to get back to their daily social and professional activities without revealing signs of a completed operation. The majority of women also prefer the Unshaven Follicular Unit Extraction, as shaving the donor area is generally unacceptable in ladies.

        What is the result expected after an FUE hair transplant?

        The method of Follicular Unit Extraction provides absolutely permanent and natural results. The results after an FUE hair transplantation can vary from satisfactory to excellent depending on the doctor’s experience and training. Actually, the growth of new hair is usually over 95% and a totally natural result is expected only when a specialized doctor performs hair transplant with the FUE method.

        The growth of the new hair is achieved gradually after the transplantation and the final result is completed within the first 12 months. No linear scars are left behind as the method is minimally invasive. The majority of hair is preserved and starts growing from the very first days post-operatively. Some hair might fall off during the next 2 to 6 weeks since they fall in the telogen phase of their hair growth cycle. The gradual regrowth of the new hair follows a process that is completed after 10-12 months post-operatively. Growth of new hair has been recorded even after 18-24 months. It is interesting to underline the fact that when hair implantation has been applied in the hair line and facial area (for example beard transplant, eyebrow transplant) hair growth is faster because of the rich blood supply to these body areas. The growth reaches 80% within the first 4-6 months after the application of FUE hair transplant.

        For those that want to achieve the greatest possible density or wish to cover larger areas, as it happens in the case of advanced alopecia, two-day sessions are usually required. The minimally invasive nature of Follicular Unit Extraction method in comparison to FUT-Strip technique, enables the usage of the donor area in a future hair transplant.

        What is the cost of an FUE hair transplant session?

        The cost of the sessions may vary, depending on patients’ needs. Elements that form the final cost are the duration of the session, the number of the transferred hair follicles, the presence of scars as a result of a former Strip operation, the option of having an unshaven FUE session. An unshaven FUE session lasts longer and demands a great level of skillfulness on behalf of the performing doctor. The cost of eyebrows or scars’ restoration depends on the level of the session’s extent, and the degree of difficulty of the case.
